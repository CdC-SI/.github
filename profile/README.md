# Central Compensation Office — Open Source

**Information Systems Division | Swiss Confederation**

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the Information Systems Division (CdC-SI) of the Swiss Central Compensation Office (CCO).

## About us

The Central Compensation Office (*Centrale de compensation*, CdC / *Zentrale Ausgleichsstelle*, ZAS) is part of the Swiss Federal Administration, operating under the Federal Finance Administration (FFA).

Based in Geneva, the CCO plays a central role in the Swiss first-pillar social security system, including Old-Age and Survivors' Insurance (OASI), Disability Insurance (DI), and Loss of Earnings Compensation (APG).

Our Information Systems Division designs, develops and maintains digital solutions supporting these public services.

Learn more about our institution: [zas.admin.ch](https://www.zas.admin.ch/en).

## Our commitment to Open Source

We believe that publicly funded software can create value beyond the organization for which it was originally developed.

In accordance with Article 9 of the Swiss Federal Act on the Use of Electronic Means to Carry Out Official Tasks (EMOTA / LMETA / EMBAG), we contribute to making software developed for public administration available as open source, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

Through this GitHub organization, we aim to:

- Promote transparency and software reuse.
- Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing.
- Contribute to the Swiss Confederation's open-source ecosystem.
- Share technical solutions and innovations developed within our organization.

## Our projects

Our public repositories include software projects, experimental solutions, and reusable components.

Some of our projects:

- [ZAS/EAK CopilotGPT](https://github.com/CdC-SI/ZAS-EAK-CopilotGPT) — An AI assistant project developed during Innovation Fellowship 2024.
- [ZIA Translation](https://github.com/CdC-SI/zia-translation) — A document translation and data extraction service.
- [ZIA RAG](https://github.com/CdC-SI/zia-rag) — A retrieval service supporting vector and full-text search.

Explore [all our public repositories](https://github.com/orgs/CdC-SI/repositories).

Projects may have different maturity levels and maintenance policies.

## Licensing and contributions

Licensing terms are defined at repository level. Please consult the relevant license and documentation before reusing or modifying any project.

Contributions, issue reporting and support arrangements depend on each project's governance and available resources.

For security concerns, please follow the repository's security reporting instructions where available. Do not disclose sensitive information in public issues.

## Important information

Our public repositories contain software source code, not operational data or access to the CCO's internal systems.

Software published here is provided under its respective license terms. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these repositories are not official channels for administrative requests or support regarding social insurance services.

For official services and information, please visit [zas.admin.ch](https://www.zas.admin.ch/en).
